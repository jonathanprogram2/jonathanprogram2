## 🧠 About Me

I'm an **AI engineering and automation developer** based in **Cleveland, Ohio**, with a background in computer science and a growing focus on building, evaluating, and integrating LLM-powered systems.

I work at the intersection of **AI agents, LLM evaluation, workflow automation, backend development, and human-centered interfaces**. I enjoy taking an AI idea beyond a prompt: connecting models to tools and APIs, defining measurable evaluations, handling failure cases, and creating software that makes the system's behavior understandable.

**A few things about me:**

- 🤖 **AI agents & evaluation:** Build tool-calling workflows and automated tests; work professionally on SWE-agent coding benchmarks and execution-trace reviews.
- ⚙️ **AI automation:** Design event-driven workflows with LLM classification, REST/webhook integrations, human review, and audit trails.
- 🐍 **Software development:** Build with Python, FastAPI, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, and Next.js.
- 🌐 **Open source:** Contribute to React/TypeScript projects and work through maintainer reviews and CI checks.
- 🎓 **Education:** Pursuing an **M.S. in Human-Computer Interaction at Rochester Institute of Technology**; earned a **B.S. in Computer Science (AI concentration)** from Full Sail University as **Valedictorian**.
- 🏢 **Independent development:** Building web applications and interfaces through **[JAMX Studios](https://jamxstudios.vercel.app)**.
